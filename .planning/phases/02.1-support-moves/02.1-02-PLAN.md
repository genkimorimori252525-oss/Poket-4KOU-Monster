Give the 技クリエーター (`src/creator.tpl.html`) a working way to author `kind`/`effect`: an
攻撃/補助 toggle that actually rebuilds the editor when switched, effect inputs (stat/delta/dur/

next time the individual loads.

Purpose: design doc §4.5-4, item 5 of 6. Without this, `kind:'support'` can only be set by hand-
editing JSON, and worse, even a hand-edited support move would have its `power` resurrected on the
very next load — making it deal real damage AND have the checker's `期待ダメージ` bonus AND the
補助の値打ち term simultaneously, which is a lie in the AI log (掟9).

Output: a working 補助技 authoring flow, a `normalizeMon()` that can't corrupt it, and
`tools/verify_creator.js` extended to prove the round-trip via actual DOM behavior.
